War in Ukraine — Lead Story

Missiles hit Zelenskyy’s home town: 2 killed, 60 hurt in Kryvyi Rih

Russian ballistic missiles struck the centre of Kryvyi Rih on Friday night — then hit rescuers as they worked. A 7-year-old boy and a 20-year-old woman were killed; 60 people were wounded, six of them children. Fires burned and about 25 apartment blocks, a hospital and schools were damaged.

Mexico

“El Chapo Isidro” captured; 11 dead in Sinaloa gun battles

Troops seized FBI most-wanted cartel boss Fausto Isidro Meza Flores in Guasave. Four soldiers and seven gunmen were killed; burning trucks blocked roads.

Space

BepiColombo reaches Mercury after eight years in space

The ESA–JAXA probe fired its thrusters and slipped into orbit around Mercury — only the second mission ever to do so. Science begins in April 2027.

Nigeria

Funeral boat capsizes in Bayelsa; at least 24 dead

A boat carrying mourners to a burial capsized in Bayelsa State after a mechanical fault. At least 24 people died; 24 were rescued as the search went on.

United States

Trump vows 10% cap on credit-card interest rates

President Trump said he will work with the incoming Democratic Congress to cap card rates at 10% — down from an average above 22%. No bill has passed yet.

Europe — NATO

NATO vows to defend “every inch” of Poland after Russian missiles land there

Secretary General Mark Rutte, in Warsaw a day after two Russian missiles and a drone came down in Poland, pledged the alliance would defend Polish territory — and said NATO’s council will meet on Monday.
